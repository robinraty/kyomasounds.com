About

The story behind Kyoma Sounds — from high-energy stages to peaceful lofi soundscapes.

The beginning

From electronic music to emotional storytelling

I started making music years before Kyoma existed, exploring electronic sounds, textures and energy through my first project, Roja. At that time, music was intense, colorful and deeply connected to live moments, festivals and late-night inspiration.

It taught me how powerful sound can be — not only as entertainment, but as a way to express emotions that are sometimes difficult to explain with words.

Kyoma Sounds

A quiet refuge through lofi and asian-inspired sounds

Kyoma was born from a softer place. Over time, I felt drawn to calmer music — warm melodies, nostalgic textures, gentle rhythms and asian-inspired instruments. Those sounds bring me a kind of peace I always come back to.

Today, Kyoma Sounds is about creating relaxing music for quiet moments: studying, walking, resting, healing or simply slowing down. A peaceful world where emotions can breathe.
