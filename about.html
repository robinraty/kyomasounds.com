About

From electronic music to peaceful lofi, ambient textures and quiet moments.

The beginning

BEFORE KYOMA

I started making music years before Kyoma existed, under the name Roja, my first psytrance project.

It was a very different world: more intense, more energetic, and closely connected to live sets, festivals and late-night inspiration.

That period taught me how powerful sound can be. Not only as entertainment, but as a way to express emotions that are hard to explain with words.

Kyoma Sounds

A SOFTER PLACE

After years of intense electronic music and late nights, I slowly moved toward sounds that felt more peaceful and personal.

I started looking for music that felt more connected to my inner world: something softer, more introspective, and closer to the way I was changing as a person.

Kyoma became a way to turn that need for peace into sound.

KYOMA

Relaxing sounds for quiet moments and inner peace.
